How it is stated

This policy is stated through robots.txt (Content-Signal and Content-Usage lines), /.well-known/tdmrep.json, and the IPTC Data Mining field inside each image file.

Search engines and AI assistants are welcome to index, quote, summarize and link.

What this does not do

  • robots.txt is a request. A US court called it “precatory.” Only a blocking rule at the edge, such as Cloudflare’s bot blocking, or on a server, turns a crawler away.
  • Google’s AI Overviews use Google’s search crawler, so robots.txt cannot refuse them without leaving Google search. Google-Extended covers training only. Bing’s noarchive tag keeps a page out of Copilot answers while it stays in Bing search.
  • GitHub Pages cannot send custom headers: the noai image header and the TDM headers are not possible there. The meta tags and /.well-known/tdmrep.json still work.
  • Microsoft publishes no image-only training control; Bing image training is not refusable by this preset.
  • No major AI company has published that it reads the TDM reservation or the in-file IPTC field; their published controls are robots.txt and, for Microsoft, a page tag (noarchive or nocache). This is not legal advice.
