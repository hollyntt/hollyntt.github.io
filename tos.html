1. General

By commissioning me, you agree to the terms on this page. These terms may be updated at any time; the version live at the time you commission is the one that applies to your order.

Turnaround is dependent on my available time. This isn't my full-time job, so progress on your commission may not be continuous — I'll keep you updated, but please don't expect an immediate or fixed completion date.

2. What I Take Commissions For

I currently only take commissions for VRChat avatar kitbashes — specifically, things I've already learned and am confident doing well. Right now that's:

  • Head swaps
  • Ear swaps

More services will be added to this list (and the price sheet) as I learn them. I won't take on a kitbash type that isn't listed here yet.

4. Payment

  • I only accept Cash App and PayPal.
  • For PayPal, the final invoiced amount may be adjusted from the listed price to account for your region and any applicable sales tax/PayPal fees — this is calculated per-order at invoice time, not a flat markup. Cash App payments are quoted at the listed price.
  • PayPal payments must be sent as Friends & Family, not Goods & Services. Cash App has no equivalent requirement.
  • Payment is 50% upfront, 50% on completion for every 3D model commission (see Process below for why).

5. Process & Timeline

  • The first 50% is paid upfront before I start work.
  • While the piece is in progress, that initial payment covers edits and adjustments as we go back and forth — nothing is "locked in" until you're happy with it.
  • Once the model is finished and you're satisfied, the remaining 50% is due to finalize and deliver the completed file.
  • Slot availability and status are tracked on the commissions board.

6. Usage & Ownership

Kitbashes are built using other people's hard work — base models, existing avatar assets, and my own edits layered on top. Because of that:

  • The finished kitbash cannot be resold to anyone else.
  • It cannot be minted as an NFT or similar token.
  • It cannot be used for AI training of any kind.
  • None of the above is permitted without my written permission, given case-by-case.

7. Refunds & Cancellations

  • Full refund of the upfront 50% if I haven't started work yet.
  • Partial refund (minus time already spent) if cancelled after work has started.
  • The final 50% is only collected once you've confirmed you're happy with the finished result, so it is non-refundable once paid.

8. Content I Won't Make

  • Hate symbols, real-world hate groups, or content promoting bigotry.
  • Kitbashes involving stolen/DMCA'd base models or assets you don't have rights to use.
  • Anything sexualizing minors, in any form.

9. Contact & Board

Open slots, queue status, and how to request a commission all live on the commissions board. For questions, reach me on Discord.
